## Short description

Read your existing daily notes as a read-only journal timeline with infinite
scroll, on desktop and mobile. Uses your Daily notes or Periodic Notes settings.
Search all notes or jump to a date.

## Long description

Daily Notes Stream shows every daily note on one page, like a journal you scroll
through. There's no new folder, format, or template to set up: it reads the
notes your Daily notes or Periodic Notes settings already point to. The newest
day sits at the top, and older days load as you scroll, so you can read back
through years of notes without the stream slowing down. Each note appears in
full, just as in reading view, with its images, tables, embeds, Bases, Dataview
tables, properties, and linked mentions. When you want to change something,
click a date to open that note, and the stream updates as you edit. To find your
way around, search every note at once or jump straight to any date. Sections you
fold stay folded, and empty notes that hold only your template, from Templates
or Templater, can be hidden. On phones and tablets, wide tables and Bases turn
into cards, so one swipe always moves the whole stream. It never changes your
notes or collects any data.
